## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, ultra-clean visual standard engineered for high-level governance, institutional endowment, and corporate leadership aligned with Saudi Vision 2030. It rejects superficial ornamentation, faux metallics, and artificial luxury signifiers in favor of rigorous structural precision, pure whitespace, and disciplined chromatic layering.

The visual language draws inspiration from modern monumental architecture: orthogonal lines, sharp right angles, unyielding geometry, and airy vertical proportion. Built natively for Right-to-Left (RTL) reading patterns, every element communicates stability, institutional endurance (*Rawasi*), and forward-looking clarity.

### Personality & Atmosphere
- **Institutional Weight:** Immovable, sober, and authoritative without feeling archaic or heavy.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Strict rectilinear structures, zero-radius geometries, and fine-line borders create an environment of transparent governance.
- **Pristine Radiance:** Pure white fields punctuated by cool sapphire gradients and pale ice surfaces evoke daylight, maritime horizon, and high-altitude perspective.
- **Restraint:** High visual impact is achieved entirely through typographic scale, razor-sharp alignment, and calculated rhythmic spacing.

## Colors

The palette is strictly restricted to light mode. It eliminates dark backdrops, muddy earth tones, and warm metallics entirely, relying on a calibrated gradient of cool blues anchored against immaculate white and crisp ice tones.

### Color Roles & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** The primary canvas. Unbroken, bright, and deliberate.
- **Surface Tint (`#F2F8FC`):** A barely perceptible ice-blue wash used for secondary structural zones, tabular alternate rows, and distinct modular containers.
- **Structural Border (`#E2ECF5`):** A hairline boundary tone used to define zero-radius frames and editorial grids without visual clutter.
- **Secondary Tone — Deep Royal Navy (`#163063`):** The foundational anchor color. Assigned to headline typography, primary call-to-action fills, monumental structural dividers, and bottom governance zones.
- **Primary Tone — Classic Corporate Blue (`#2360A5`):** The core active brand hue. Drives active interactive states, primary iconography, pillar keyframes, and focal data metrics.
- **Accent Highlight — Bright Sky Cerulean (`#3488C4`):** Represents secondary pillars, subtle focus halos, contextual badges, and data visualization overlays.
- **Tertiary Accent — Soft Turquoise Teal (`#47A3B8`):** Denotes tertiary initiatives, environmental metrics, micro status ribbons, and structural hairline accents.
- **Neutral Typography (`#526071`):** A balanced slate-gray designed for maximum legibility against white and ice backdrops, avoiding the harsh optical vibration of pure black while retaining crisp RTL letterform definition.

## Typography

The typography system pairs formal editorial prestige with technical clarity across all screen sizes.

### Typeface Hierarchy
- **Noto Serif (Headlines & Institutional Statements):** Imparts generational weight and classical governance. It anchors landing page hero statements, section headings, and keynote quotes.
- **IBM Plex Sans (Body, Navigation & Metadata):** A rationalist, humanist sans-serif providing unparalleled clarity in Arabic script (via its native Arabic glyph set) across high-density tables, editorial bodies, form controls, and micro-labels.

### Typographic Principles for Arabic RTL
- **Line Heights:** Arabic diacritics and ascenders/descenders require expanded vertical clearance. Line heights are calibrated between 1.6x and 1.7x for body copy to prevent collision and preserve rapid scanability.
- **Weight Calibration:** Titles employ Semi-Bold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) weights to produce decisive optical presence. Running text remains Medium (`500`) or Regular (`400`) to sustain long-form readability without ink-trap bleeding.
- **Letter Spacing:** Fixed letter spacing (`tracking`) is strictly zeroed or set to default for Arabic scripts to avoid breaking cursive ligatures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors architectural plans: generous spatial intervals, crisp alignment axes, and proportional mathematical cadence.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, enclosed by a minimum outer margin of 3rem (`48px`). Maximum content constraint is capped at 1360px to preserve comfortable optical bounds.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column grid with 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 1.25rem (`20px`) margins.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Section Pacing:** Institutional sections transition across expansive vertical gaps (`space-3xl` / 96px on desktop) to communicate scale and structural calm.
- **Module Nesting:** Related metadata and controls use tight, predictable pacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`), creating self-contained data clusters that contrast sharply against the surrounding spatial vacuum.
- **RTL Symmetrical Padding:** Lateral interior paddings across containers are strictly identical on the start and end edges to ensure balanced bidirectional rendering.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional heavy shadows, skeuomorphism, and blurred glass layers. Instead, spatial hierarchy and depth are established entirely through **tonal planes** and **micro-hairline borders**.

### Structural Layering
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Pure `#FFFFFF`. The underlying ground plane across all pages.
2. **Level 1 (Sub-Surface Plinths):** Solid `#F2F8FC` containers bounded by an exact 1px `#E2ECF5` stroke. Used for modular content grids, statistic groupings, and executive panels.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements):** Dropdown menus, modal dialogs, and popovers utilize pure white backgrounds enclosed in a crisp 1px stroke of `#2360A5` (at 20% opacity) accompanied by an ultra-subtle, non-intrusive shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(22, 48, 99, 0.06)`).

### Line Accents
Visual prominence is directed using sharp, high-contrast directional lines rather than elevation:
- Primary cards incorporate a 2px top or right border in `#2360A5` or `#47A3B8`.
- Section dividers use single 1px continuous hairline rules in `#E2ECF5` running from boundary to boundary.

## Shapes

The geometric rule is absolute: **Sharp (`0px` corner radius)** across every visible UI element.

### Architectural Rationale
- Buttons, inputs, modal containers, image masks, tags, and cards terminate in clean 90-degree right angles.
- Sharp geometry conveys institutional permanence, judicial and administrative precision, and architectural discipline.
- Curves and pills are banned; all circular forms are restricted strictly to specialized status dots, standard bullet indicators, or icon vector paths.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid background of Deep Royal Navy (`#163063`), crisp white text, 0px border-radius, minimum height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Hover state transitions to Classic Corporate Blue (`#2360A5`). Active state exhibits a 1px interior inset border.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pure white background, 1px solid border in `#163063`, text color `#163063`. Hover state shifts surface to `#F2F8FC`.
- **Text Link CTA:** Inline link in `#2360A5` with a persistent or hover-triggered 1px underline, paired with an RTL trailing arrow (`←`).

### Cards & Modular Plinths
- Rectangular 0px containers with a `#FFFFFF` or `#F2F8FC` foundation.
- Enclosed by a uniform 1px `#E2ECF5` border.
- Optional architectural accent: an asymmetrical 3px top or right border in Deep Royal Navy (`#163063`) or Bright Sky Cerulean (`#3488C4`) denoting pillar affiliation.
- No floating hover elevations; interactive cards signal hover via a border color transition to `#2360A5`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base State:** 0px sharp rectangular fields, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2ECF5`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Text aligned to the right.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#2360A5` with no diffuse glow; clean mechanical edge definition.
- **Labels:** Positioned above the field in IBM Plex Sans Semi-Bold (`#163063`), accompanied by small metadata hints in `#526071`.

### Data Chips & Badges
- Square-cut rectangular tags (0px border-radius).
- Background `#F2F8FC`, border 1px solid `#E2ECF5`, text `#163063`.
- Pillar variants leverage tinted text and 1px border accents derived from Pillar 2 (`#3488C4`) or Pillar 3 (`#47A3B8`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Crisp 18x18px square (0px radius), 1px border in `#526071`. When checked, solid `#163063` fill with an optical white glyph.
- **Radios:** Outer 18x18px crisp square frame with a 6x6px inner square indicator (maintaining the zero-radius aesthetic throughout state indicators).

### Lists & Navigation
- Primary navigation aligns horizontally across the top right axis with generous `space-lg` item spacing.
- Active menu states are signaled by a 2px horizontal indicator line placed flush against the header boundary in `#2360A5`.
- Secondary lists feature hairline bottom dividers in `#E2ECF5` with content indented cleanly from the right margin.
